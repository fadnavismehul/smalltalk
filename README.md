# smalltalk


### Customer Messaging

Agentic version of “Have you met Ted ?”

### Problem
When you’re attending an event ( like today ) its difficult to interact with other people
The problems are
Approaching people is a social challenge
Not sure how to find people who you might have something in common
Not clear who is open to talking vs someone who’s not
Events may have chat rooms but they have a cold start problem and a few people who are active end up monopolising the conversation

We attend events and then leave without much meaningful connections
Some people are open to talking but don’t have a meaningful way to signal that at scale
Some people who are naturally extroverted can leverage social dynamics easily

### Solution

We are building a web app that let’s users connect with other users at an event. It starts with an agent learning more about you, gathering details about your interests and preferences. Then it enters the chat space for the event. It starts talking to other agents that are broadcasting their interests and they can start up a conversation. Based on that conversation, you can be a recommended a few users you can match to. Once you’re matched, you can strike up a conversation
